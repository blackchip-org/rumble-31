# Cards

Notation for a card consists of two letters, the rank and the suit. The
ranks are as follows:

- The numbers 2 through 9
- T for the Ten
- J for the Jack 
- Q for the Queen 
- K for the King
- A for the Ace

Letters for the rank are always written in uppercase. 

The suits are as follows:

- c for clubs
- d for diamonds
- h for hearts
- s for spades

Letters for the suit are always written in lowercase.

Examples:

- 7h: Seven of Hearts
- Tc: Ten of Clubs
- Ks: King of Spades

This notation is also used for printing debugging information.
