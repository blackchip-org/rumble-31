# Rules of Rumble 31

Rumble 31 is a card game played with a deck of cards based on a standard
52-card deck of playing cards. The deck for this game consists of all cards
of the following ranks:

- 7 through 10
- All face cards
- All aces

Other cards are removed and not used for this game. 

A game always starts with four players, seated 0 through 3. As in
Bridge, each seat also has a cardinal name: seat 0 is South, seat 1 is
West, seat 2 is North, and seat 3 is East. Play proceeds clockwise
through this order: South, West, North, East.

A game begins with a randomly chosen seat as the dealer. Starting with
the second round, the dealer moves to the next seat clockwise from the
previous round's dealer, skipping any seat eliminated from the game.

At the beginning of each round, the deck is shuffled, three cards are
dealt face-down to each player, and three cards are dealt to a common
pool of cards called the pot. Play starts with the seat immediately
clockwise of the dealer.

The pot is private when it is dealt: only the player who acts first
that round may see it. Once that player has taken their turn, the pot
becomes public and every player can see it for the rest of the round.

Because only the first player to act can see the pot, their turn is
different from every other turn in the round. They have exactly two
actions available:

- Take Pot: exchange all cards from their hand with the pot; or
- Keep: leave their hand as dealt.

Neither Take Pot nor Keep counts as a knock. Trading a single card is
not available on the round's first turn.

From the round's second turn on, every player's actions are to either:

- Trade one card from their hand with a card from the pot; 
- Exchange all cards from their hand with the pot; or
- Knock

Exchanging all cards is also a knock. Knocking itself is legal on any
turn from the round's second turn on.

When the turn is over, play continues to the next player in clockwise order. 

The round ends when:

- The next time it would be a player's turn if they knocked
- Immediately when a player has three aces in their hand (a score of
  32), including at the deal
- Immediately when a player's action leaves their hand scoring 31,
  unless it was that player's own first turn of the round
- Immediately at the start of a player's second turn, before they
  act, if their hand already scores 31 (having reached it during
  their first turn, which is not checked until now)

A hand can only reach a score of 31 or 32 through the scoring rules
below; checking a player's hand for either of these scores a third
time or later is never triggered, since the checks above would
already have ended the round by then.

Scores are then tallied for each player using the following rules:

- Number cards are worth their pip value
- Face cards are worth 10 points
- Aces are worth 11 points

The total point value is the highest sum of cards that belong to the same suit.
The exceptions to this rule are:

- If a player has three ace cards, the score is 32. 
- If a player has three cards of the same rank (not aces), the score is 30.5

Examples:

- 7h Ad Kh: The total is 17 (7 + K)
- 7h Ad Kc: The total is 11 (A) 
- 7h 7d 7c: The total is 30.5
- Ah Ad Ac: The total is 32

A game is played over a series of rounds. When a round ends, the player
with the lowest score receives a strike. If more than one player is tied
for the lowest score, they all receive strikes. A player with three strikes
is removed from the game: starting with the next round, they are no
longer dealt a hand, do not take turns, and do not appear in round
results or the pot. The last remaining player is the winner of the
game.
